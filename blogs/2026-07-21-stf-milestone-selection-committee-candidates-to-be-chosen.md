A small group of 5 community members have stepped forward to volunteer as STF Milestone Selection Committee members. These are the volunteers who will be interviewing and selecting future candidates for paid positions on STF milestone teams. If you are an active, official member, team lead, or assistant team lead, please watch the inbox of the email account registered with your volunteer portal record.
